# Spanish A0 Course Outline

This document is a human-readable course outline for Spanish A0. It is an authoring aid for planning the course sequence and future content work.

This is an outline, not a blueprint. General course rules and educational boundaries are defined in `docs/LANGUAGE_COURSE_BLUEPRINT.md`. The machine-readable course structure is stored in `spanish_a0_course.json`.

The outline describes what each unit and lesson should cover so that future curriculum, LessonDefinition, EducationalContent, vocabulary, grammar, dialogues, readings, and exercise templates can be authored consistently.

---

## Unit 1: First Contact

**Unit goal:** Help the learner recognize and use the first social phrases needed to start and end a very simple interaction.

**Communicative scope:** Greetings, farewells, politeness, basic classroom survival, and asking for repetition.

### Lesson 1: Hello and Goodbye

- **Lesson goal:** Recognize and use the most common greetings and farewells.
- **New vocabulary:** hola, buenos dias, buenas tardes, buenas noches, adios.
- **New grammar:** No new grammar beyond fixed phrases.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can greet someone and say goodbye in a basic exchange.

### Lesson 2: Please, Thank You, Sorry

- **Lesson goal:** Use simple polite expressions in short interactions.
- **New vocabulary:** por favor, gracias, perdon, de nada.
- **New grammar:** Fixed politeness formulas.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can make a tiny request politely and respond to thanks.

### Lesson 3: Yes, No, I Do Not Understand

- **Lesson goal:** Recognize essential clarification phrases.
- **New vocabulary:** si, no, no entiendo, repite, mas despacio.
- **New grammar:** Basic negation with no as a fixed pattern.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can signal understanding or ask for slower repetition.

### Lesson 4: Nice to Meet You

- **Lesson goal:** Combine a greeting with a first social response.
- **New vocabulary:** mucho gusto, igualmente, senor, senora.
- **New grammar:** Fixed response patterns for introductions.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can respond naturally after meeting someone.

## Unit 2: Saying Who You Are

**Unit goal:** Give the learner a minimal introduction pattern using name, basic identity, and very limited personal pronouns.

**Communicative scope:** Saying a name, asking for a name, using yo/tu, and recognizing simple introduction patterns.

### Lesson 1: My Name Is

- **Lesson goal:** Give a name using a memorized introduction pattern.
- **New vocabulary:** me llamo, nombre, Ana, Luis.
- **New grammar:** llamarse in first person as a fixed pattern.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can say "Me llamo..." and understand the same pattern from another speaker.

### Lesson 2: What Is Your Name?

- **Lesson goal:** Ask and answer a simple name question.
- **New vocabulary:** como, te llamas, tu.
- **New grammar:** Question intonation and the pattern Como te llamas?
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can ask for someone's name and answer with their own.

### Lesson 3: I Am

- **Lesson goal:** Use soy for a very short identity statement.
- **New vocabulary:** soy, estudiante, persona, amigo, amiga.
- **New grammar:** ser in first person only, plus basic gender awareness in amigo/amiga.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can say a tiny sentence about identity, such as "Soy estudiante."

### Lesson 4: You Are

- **Lesson goal:** Recognize and use eres in very simple statements and questions.
- **New vocabulary:** eres, tambien.
- **New grammar:** ser in second person singular only, with yo and tu.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can recognize "Tu eres..." and answer simple yes/no identity questions.

## Unit 3: Origin, City, and Language

**Unit goal:** Let the learner share where they are from and what language they speak in very simple phrases.

**Communicative scope:** Country, city, nationality as recognition only, language ability, and basic personal exchange.

### Lesson 1: I Am From

- **Lesson goal:** Give basic origin information.
- **New vocabulary:** de, pais, Espana, Mexico, Ucrania, Estados Unidos.
- **New grammar:** soy de as a fixed origin pattern.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can say "Soy de..." and understand the same information from another person.

### Lesson 2: Where Are You From?

- **Lesson goal:** Ask a simple question about origin.
- **New vocabulary:** donde, eres, de donde eres.
- **New grammar:** Question pattern De donde eres?
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can ask where someone is from and answer with a country.

### Lesson 3: My City

- **Lesson goal:** Name a city and recognize the phrase "vivo en" as a fixed expression.
- **New vocabulary:** ciudad, vivo en, Madrid, Barcelona, Kyiv.
- **New grammar:** vivo en as an unanalyzed useful phrase.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can say the city where they live using a simple fixed pattern.

### Lesson 4: I Speak a Little Spanish

- **Lesson goal:** Describe very basic language ability.
- **New vocabulary:** hablo, espanol, ingles, un poco, no hablo.
- **New grammar:** hablar in first person only, plus basic no before a verb.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can say they speak a little Spanish or do not speak a language.

## Unit 4: Numbers and Basic Personal Details

**Unit goal:** Build the smallest useful number base and connect it to age, phone-like digits, and simple personal information.

**Communicative scope:** Numbers 0-20, age as a fixed expression, spelling or confirming simple details.

### Lesson 1: Numbers 0-10

- **Lesson goal:** Recognize and use numbers from zero to ten.
- **New vocabulary:** cero, uno, dos, tres, cuatro, cinco, seis, siete, ocho, nueve, diez.
- **New grammar:** No new grammar.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can understand and say single digits and small quantities.

### Lesson 2: Numbers 11-20

- **Lesson goal:** Extend number recognition to twenty.
- **New vocabulary:** once, doce, trece, catorce, quince, dieciseis, diecisiete, dieciocho, diecinueve, veinte.
- **New grammar:** No new grammar.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can recognize small numbers used in everyday contexts.

### Lesson 3: I Am ... Years Old

- **Lesson goal:** Say age using a fixed tener expression.
- **New vocabulary:** tengo, anos, cuantos, cuantos anos tienes.
- **New grammar:** tener in first and second person only for age.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can ask and answer a simple age question.

### Lesson 4: Basic Details

- **Lesson goal:** Combine name, origin, language, and a number in one short personal profile.
- **New vocabulary:** telefono, numero, mi.
- **New grammar:** mi as a basic possessive before a known noun.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can understand and produce a tiny personal-information exchange.

## Unit 5: People and Family

**Unit goal:** Introduce immediate family vocabulary and the first noun gender and article patterns.

**Communicative scope:** Naming close family members, saying who someone is, and recognizing singular/plural at a basic level.

### Lesson 1: Family Words

- **Lesson goal:** Recognize and use immediate family nouns.
- **New vocabulary:** familia, madre, padre, hermana, hermano.
- **New grammar:** Basic masculine and feminine noun patterns.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can identify close family members with single words or short phrases.

### Lesson 2: My Family

- **Lesson goal:** Use mi with family nouns.
- **New vocabulary:** mi madre, mi padre, mi hermana, mi hermano.
- **New grammar:** mi with singular family nouns.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can say "mi madre", "mi hermano", and similar short phrases.

### Lesson 3: Articles: el and la

- **Lesson goal:** Recognize basic definite articles with known nouns.
- **New vocabulary:** el, la, hombre, mujer.
- **New grammar:** el/la with masculine and feminine singular nouns.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can match common nouns with el or la in controlled contexts.

### Lesson 4: One or More

- **Lesson goal:** Notice basic singular and plural forms.
- **New vocabulary:** los, las, amigos, amigas.
- **New grammar:** Basic plural -s and definite plural articles.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can recognize when a known noun refers to one person or more than one.

## Unit 6: Objects and Everyday Needs

**Unit goal:** Give the learner simple words and patterns for immediate everyday situations without expanding grammar too quickly.

**Communicative scope:** Common objects, basic needs, asking where something is, and using estar only for location.

### Lesson 1: Common Objects

- **Lesson goal:** Learn a small set of useful object nouns.
- **New vocabulary:** agua, cafe, pan, libro, telefono, bano.
- **New grammar:** un/una as basic indefinite articles.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can name or recognize a few useful everyday items.

### Lesson 2: I Need

- **Lesson goal:** Express a basic immediate need.
- **New vocabulary:** necesito, quiero, agua, ayuda.
- **New grammar:** necesitar and querer in first person only as fixed patterns.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can say "Necesito ayuda" or "Quiero agua" in a simple context.

### Lesson 3: Where Is It?

- **Lesson goal:** Ask and understand a simple location question.
- **New vocabulary:** donde esta, aqui, alli.
- **New grammar:** estar in third person singular for location only.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can ask where a place or object is and understand a short answer.

### Lesson 4: Basic Places

- **Lesson goal:** Recognize the most useful place words for survival situations.
- **New vocabulary:** bano, hotel, estacion, casa, escuela.
- **New grammar:** en as a basic location preposition.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can understand and produce very short location phrases.

## Unit 7: Simple Questions and Present Actions

**Unit goal:** Add the smallest useful set of question words and high-frequency present-tense verbs.

**Communicative scope:** Asking what/where/who, saying what one has, wants, needs, and does in minimal phrases.

### Lesson 1: What Is This?

- **Lesson goal:** Ask and answer a simple identification question.
- **New vocabulary:** que, esto, es, cosa.
- **New grammar:** Que es esto? and Es... as fixed patterns.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can ask what something is and understand a short answer.

### Lesson 2: Who Is It?

- **Lesson goal:** Ask who someone is using a controlled pattern.
- **New vocabulary:** quien, el, ella.
- **New grammar:** el/ella as subject pronouns; es for basic identification.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can ask "Quien es?" and answer with a name or simple role.

### Lesson 3: I Have / I Want / I Need

- **Lesson goal:** Compare three high-frequency first-person present-tense phrases.
- **New vocabulary:** tengo, quiero, necesito, no tengo.
- **New grammar:** First-person present forms as practical chunks; no before the verb.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can state a possession, wish, or need in a very short sentence.

### Lesson 4: Review of Basic Questions

- **Lesson goal:** Reuse Como, Donde, Que, and Quien with known vocabulary.
- **New vocabulary:** No major new vocabulary; recycle names, places, objects, and family words.
- **New grammar:** Basic question-word recognition and question intonation.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can choose the right basic question for a familiar situation.

## Unit 8: A0 Consolidation

**Unit goal:** Consolidate the whole A0 course into short, realistic exchanges about self, family, origin, numbers, and immediate needs.

**Communicative scope:** Mini-dialogues, short personal profile, simple requests, and readiness for early A1 content.

### Lesson 1: My Mini Profile

- **Lesson goal:** Combine name, origin, language, and age in a short self-introduction.
- **New vocabulary:** No major new vocabulary; recycle personal-information language.
- **New grammar:** Review llamarse, ser, tener, hablar, and basic negation in learned patterns.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can give a four-line personal introduction using familiar structures.

### Lesson 2: My Family and City

- **Lesson goal:** Reuse family and city vocabulary in short sentences.
- **New vocabulary:** No major new vocabulary; recycle family, city, and place words.
- **New grammar:** Review mi, el/la, los/las, and basic plural recognition.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can say a few simple facts about family and city.

### Lesson 3: In a Simple Situation

- **Lesson goal:** Use survival phrases for a tiny everyday scenario.
- **New vocabulary:** No major new vocabulary; recycle needs, places, and politeness.
- **New grammar:** Review necesito, quiero, donde esta, and en as controlled patterns.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can ask for help, ask where something is, or state an immediate need.

### Lesson 4: A0 Checkpoint

- **Lesson goal:** Confirm readiness to move from A0 foundations into early A1 work.
- **New vocabulary:** No new vocabulary.
- **New grammar:** No new grammar; review only.
- **Communicative outcome:** The learner can handle a very short guided exchange using greetings, personal details, family, numbers, basic questions, and everyday needs.

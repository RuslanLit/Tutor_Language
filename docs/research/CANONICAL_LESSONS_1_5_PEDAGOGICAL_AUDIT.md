# Canonical Spanish A0 Lessons 1–5: Pedagogical Audit

Status: EVIDENCE
Scope: Spanish course/pedagogical review evidence
Normative authority: PEDAGOGICAL_SCENARIO_MODEL.md

## Sequence audit

The five lessons retain the existing module objective: first contact, more
personal identity information, polite exchange, introducing another person,
and sustained questions about that person. The revision treats them as one
cumulative system.

| Lesson | Before | Revision and learner ability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A complete identity exchange was required from a zero-knowledge learner; location and language forms appeared before their later lesson. | A small first-contact nucleus is established and retrieved internally: greeting, name, name question, polite reaction, closing. |
| 2 | New identity questions began the lesson, while the Lesson 1 nucleus was mostly visible only in the final dialogue. | The Lesson 1 contact is independently reactivated first; country, city and language information then expand what the learner can do. |
| 3 | The polite forms were useful but began with a long run of recognition items and several apology/farewell variants. | The complete Lesson 2 introduction is retrieved first; the new status/thanks/repair core is inserted into that known exchange. |
| 4 | Third-person transfer was present, but earlier interaction was mainly deferred to the final task. | The Lesson 3 conversation is retrieved first; self-description patterns now do new work for a friend, teacher or colleague. |
| 5 | The final dialogue integrated the module, but the lesson began directly with third-person questions. | The Lesson 4 profile is retrieved first; new third-person questions and `y`/`pero` support a less-scaffolded information exchange. |

## Retrieval and load

The explicit first step of Lessons 2–5 references the prior lesson's
independent dialogue. This creates spaced retrieval across lesson boundaries;
the new material then occupies a smaller functional core rather than replacing
the earlier one. Lesson 1 is intentionally exempt from the cross-lesson ratio:
its internal sequence moves from meaning and recognition to guided and
independent production.

Productive new material remains concentrated around the communicative need:

- Lesson 1: greeting, self-name, name question, polite reaction and closing.
- Lesson 2: where someone lives, which languages someone speaks, addition,
  limited ability and a negative language response.
- Lesson 3: status exchange, stronger thanks, reply to thanks, repair and a
  small farewell choice.
- Lesson 4: who-question, `él`/`ella`, relationship labels and third-person
  profile frames.
- Lesson 5: third-person question set, yes/no relationship confirmation,
  `y` and `pero` for connected information.

The current deterministic engine still evaluates fixed dialogue variants and
constrained text responses. No unconstrained personal-answer task was added.

## Final performances

1. Start a short first contact.
2. Conduct an expanded introduction about self and ask the same information.
3. Conduct a polite introduction with status, thanks and repair.
4. Introduce self and another person using transferred profile language.
5. Independently ask about and describe another person in a connected exchange.

## Remaining limitation

The content engine exposes a linear reference list inside each activity, so the
new retrieval step is deterministic content rather than an adaptive tutor
conversation. Personal relevance is therefore represented through constrained
roles and named scenarios, not free answers.

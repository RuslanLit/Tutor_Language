# Spanish A1 Curriculum Blueprint

Status: PROPOSED CANONICAL A1 PLAN
Scope: curriculum intent for the Spanish A1 course that follows executable Spanish A0 Lessons 1–37
Runtime status: planning only; this document does not authorize executable Lesson 38+

This blueprint is the source of truth for authoring the first Spanish A1
lessons. `CURRICULUM_SPEC.md`, `LEARNING_MODEL.md`, `CONTENT_MODEL.md` and the
current authoring/audio standards remain authoritative for schemas, evaluation,
evidence and runtime behavior. Once A1 lessons are executable, runtime lesson
content is authoritative for what has actually been taught.

## 1. Purpose and scope

Spanish A1 extends the completed A0 foundation into short connected,
predictable everyday communication. A1 is not a second pass through A0 topics
and is not a grammar syllabus. Each module follows:

```text
communicative capability
→ high-value chunks
→ minimum grammar support
→ controlled comprehension/construction
→ typed or guided transfer
```

The course should remain offline, deterministic and usable with the current
activity architecture. Speaking practice is frequent but is never presented as
objective pronunciation mastery without ASR or another deterministic evaluator.

The lesson number is an implementation coordinate, not curriculum authority.
Future authoring must not infer pedagogy from numeric gaps, module prefixes or
stale planning numbers. Stable IDs already released in A0 must not be renamed.

## 2. A0 → A1 boundary

| Boundary | Already established or newly required behavior |
|---|---|
| A. Already A0 | greet, introduce self, ask name, state origin/residence/languages, identify people and objects, ask location, use near/far and simple directions, ask for help/services, handle basic transport, family/home, shopping/price, health/help, short practical reading, bounded guided interaction |
| B. A0 foundation → A1 expansion | reuse personal information in connected descriptions; combine location with time and plans; expand shopping into quantities/preferences/choice; reuse health/service language in longer exchanges; reuse directions in arrangements and travel; turn isolated phrases into two- or three-sentence messages |
| C. New A1 capability | describe routine and frequency; describe current activity; express likes/preferences and simple reasons; invite/accept/decline and arrange time/place; describe people and places with linked details; order food and quantities; make simple future plans; report a small completed personal event; understand short connected messages and multi-turn exchanges |

The following are not new A1 objectives: repeating `Soy de...`, isolated
`¿Dónde está...?`, or memorizing another list of shop, family or health nouns.
Those forms are substrate for the new capabilities above.

## 3. A1 exit competence

At the end of A1, a learner should be able to:

1. Give a short connected personal description including routine, interests,
   current activity and simple plans.
2. Ask for and understand practical information about time, schedules,
   availability, price, quantity, place and arrangements.
3. Express likes, dislikes, preferences and a simple reason using bounded
   familiar language.
4. Invite someone, accept or decline, and arrange a time and place.
5. Describe a familiar person, place or object with several linked details.
6. Order food or make a more complete everyday purchase using quantities and
   choices.
7. Handle a short travel, accommodation or service exchange beyond a single
   request.
8. Understand the explicit facts, sequence, time, preference and directly
   supported simple inference in short practical texts.
9. Understand short connected audio and multi-turn exchanges with familiar
   language, replaying when needed.
10. Produce bounded typed answers and short fixed-format messages of two or
    more sentences where the expected structure is deterministic.
11. Participate in predictable guided exchanges of three to five learner turns.
12. Report a simple past personal event and state a basic future intention,
    without requiring unrestricted narrative or nuanced argument.

These are communicative outcomes, not claims of certification. The final A1
checkpoint must report existing deterministic lesson/session evidence and must
not invent a separate certification subsystem.

## 4. Module sequence

The recommended course is 10 modules and 50 lessons, Lessons 38–87. Each
module has a local integration lesson or checkpoint; not every lesson is a
new-content lesson.

### Module 10 — Щоденний ритм (Lessons 38–42)

Purpose: move from isolated personal facts to describing a normal day, time and
frequency.

- Prerequisites: A0 identity, home, transport, numbers and basic questions.
- New capabilities: say what one normally does, when and how often; understand
  a simple schedule; ask about another person's routine.
- Chunks: `¿Qué haces normalmente?`, `Todos los días...`, `Por la mañana...`,
  `A veces...`, `¿A qué hora...?`.
- Grammar support: high-frequency present-tense routine verbs, reflexive
  routine chunks, time and frequency expressions.
- Vocabulary: only the routine actions, times and days needed by the scenarios;
  reuse home, transport and work/study places.
- Reading: schedules, short routine messages and 3–4 sentence daily profiles.
- Listening: short two-sentence routine descriptions, then a short question.
- Speaking: listen-repeat and guided routine rehearsal; objective evidence is
  typed/comprehension/construction.
- Recycling: identity, residence, transport, location and courtesy become
  routine context.
- Approximate size: 5 lessons.

### Module 11 — Зараз і найближчі плани (Lessons 43–47)

Purpose: distinguish habitual activity from what is happening now and from a
near-future plan.

- Prerequisites: Module 10 time/frequency and A0 `ir`/transport foundations.
- New capabilities: say what is happening now; state a near-future plan; ask
  what someone is doing and when.
- Chunks: `¿Qué haces ahora?`, `Estoy + gerundio`, `Voy a + infinitivo`,
  `Esta tarde...`, `Mañana...`.
- Grammar support: `estar + gerundio` and `ir a + infinitive` as bounded chunks;
  no full tense paradigm is required.
- Vocabulary: common current activities, days and familiar places.
- Reading: short messages with now/then contrast and simple plans.
- Listening: two- or three-sentence messages containing time and activity.
- Speaking: guided contrast of routine/current/plan.
- Recycling: routine, transport, location, help and personal information.
- Approximate size: 5 lessons.

### Module 12 — Подобається і вільний час (Lessons 48–52)

Purpose: express preferences and make simple leisure choices.

- Prerequisites: present-tense chunks, questions, basic connectors.
- New capabilities: say what one likes/dislikes, choose between options and
  give a simple reason.
- Chunks: `Me gusta...`, `No me gusta...`, `Prefiero...`, `¿Quieres...?`,
  `porque...`.
- Grammar support: gustar-type preference frames, `pero` and `porque`,
  demonstratives/quantifiers only when needed for a choice.
- Vocabulary: common leisure activities, food and familiar places; no hobby
  vocabulary dump.
- Reading: short invitations, preference messages and simple event notices.
- Listening: short choice exchanges with one explicit reason.
- Speaking: invitation rehearsal and bounded preference responses.
- Recycling: time, plans, courtesy, transport and location.
- Approximate size: 5 lessons.

### Module 13 — Їжа, меню і замовлення (Lessons 53–57)

Purpose: expand A0 shopping into a complete, bounded food transaction.

- Prerequisites: A0 price, availability, courtesy and `Necesito...`.
- New capabilities: read a simple menu, order, ask quantity/price, choose an
  option and respond to a service question.
- Chunks: `Quiero...`, `Para mí...`, `¿Cuánto cuesta...?`, `¿Cuánto...?`,
  `Un poco de...`, `La cuenta, por favor`.
- Grammar support: direct object chunks, quantifiers and singular/plural
  agreement only inside the taught transaction.
- Vocabulary: a compact menu, drinks, portions and payment terms.
- Reading: menu, order slip and short café message.
- Listening: multi-turn ordering exchange with item, quantity and price.
- Speaking: listen-repeat plus guided ordering; no ASR dependency.
- Recycling: shopping, price, help, preferences and numbers.
- Approximate size: 5 lessons.

### Module 14 — Люди, місця і деталі (Lessons 58–62)

Purpose: turn A0 descriptions into short connected descriptions of familiar
people and places.

- Prerequisites: A0 people/roles, family/home and location.
- New capabilities: describe several properties, locate details and compare
  simple alternatives.
- Chunks: `Es una persona...`, `Tiene...`, `Está...`, `Hay...`, `más... que...`,
  `Es mejor para mí`.
- Grammar support: adjective agreement in controlled contexts, `hay` versus
  `está`, simple comparison frames.
- Vocabulary: appearance/personality basics, rooms, neighborhood and useful
  public places.
- Reading: 4–5 sentence profiles, room descriptions and simple listings.
- Listening: short descriptions with two or three explicit attributes.
- Speaking: guided description with visible/bounded cues.
- Recycling: family, home, near/far, directions and personal information.
- Approximate size: 5 lessons.

### Module 15 — Запрошення і домовленості (Lessons 63–67)

Purpose: arrange a predictable meeting or activity.

- Prerequisites: time, plans, preferences, location and directions.
- New capabilities: invite, accept, decline politely, negotiate a simple time
  or place and confirm an arrangement.
- Chunks: `¿Quieres...?`, `¿Te parece...?`, `A las...`, `Nos vemos en...`,
  `Lo siento, no puedo`, `Sí, de acuerdo`.
- Grammar support: present and near-future chunks, polite negation, connectors.
- Vocabulary: meeting places, days, times and leisure activities.
- Reading: chat-like arrangements and short appointment notes.
- Listening: three-turn arrangement with one change or confirmation.
- Speaking: guided invitation and confirmation rehearsal.
- Recycling: directions, transport, preferences, courtesy and plans.
- Approximate size: 5 lessons.

### Module 16 — Подорож і проживання (Lessons 68–72)

Purpose: handle a short travel/accommodation exchange using A0 services and
new time/choice capabilities.

- Prerequisites: transport, directions, price, arrangements and requests.
- New capabilities: ask for a ticket/room, understand a schedule or booking
  detail, state destination/time and resolve one simple service problem.
- Chunks: `Quiero un billete...`, `¿A qué hora sale...?`, `Tengo una reserva`,
  `¿Hay una habitación...?`, `Necesito cambiar...`.
- Grammar support: common service frames, demonstratives/quantifiers and
  `ir a` reuse; avoid a full travel grammar set.
- Vocabulary: ticket, station, hotel, room, departure/arrival and reservation.
- Reading: timetable, booking message and hotel notice.
- Listening: short announcement or reception exchange with multiple facts.
- Speaking: guided ticket/check-in practice.
- Recycling: transport, location, price, help and arrangements.
- Approximate size: 5 lessons.

### Module 17 — Робота, навчання і повідомлення (Lessons 73–77)

Purpose: communicate a short everyday status and write bounded practical
messages.

- Prerequisites: routine, current activity, plans and descriptions.
- New capabilities: say where/when one studies or works, report availability,
  ask for a simple change and write a connected two-sentence message.
- Chunks: `Trabajo/estudio en...`, `Hoy no puedo...`, `Estoy disponible...`,
  `Te escribo porque...`, `Nos vemos después`.
- Grammar support: present-tense expansion, connectors `y`, `pero`, `porque`,
  and controlled object/place frames.
- Vocabulary: only common work/study places, tasks and message phrases.
- Reading: short email, notice and class/work message.
- Listening: voicemail-style two- or three-sentence message.
- Speaking: guided status update; recordings remain practice only.
- Recycling: routine, time, plans, invitations, help and location.
- Approximate size: 5 lessons.

### Module 18 — Учора і прості події (Lessons 78–82)

Purpose: report a small completed personal event without requiring unrestricted
storytelling.

- Prerequisites: connected messages, time expressions and familiar domains.
- New capabilities: say what happened yesterday/last weekend, state where and
  with whom, and understand a short sequence of completed events.
- Chunks: `Ayer...`, `El fin de semana...`, `Fui a...`, `Estuve en...`,
  `Comí...`, `Fue...`.
- Grammar support: a deliberately small set of high-frequency past chunks;
  introduce forms through events, not a complete preterite table.
- Vocabulary: familiar places, food, people and activities; new words only when
  required by the event sequence.
- Reading: 4–6 sentence personal note with explicit sequence.
- Listening: short connected past-event message with replay.
- Speaking: guided retelling from visual/semantic cues; not scored speech.
- Recycling: routine, plans, preferences, travel and personal information.
- Approximate size: 5 lessons.

### Module 19 — Інтеграція A1 (Lessons 83–87)

Purpose: integrate A1 capabilities and provide the final A1 checkpoint.

- Prerequisites: all previous modules.
- New capabilities: select appropriate familiar frames across time, preference,
  service, travel and personal-event contexts; sustain a short predictable
  exchange.
- Chunks: recycled only, especially `porque`, `pero`, `voy a`, `me gusta`,
  `ayer` and arrangement/service frames.
- Grammar support: integration and contrast, no new grammar in the checkpoint.
- Vocabulary: recycled course vocabulary plus only scenario glue words.
- Reading: 5–6 sentence practical messages and short multi-document tasks.
- Listening: multi-turn or connected messages with explicit replay.
- Speaking: frequent guided rehearsal, practice-only.
- Recycling: delayed retrieval of every major A0 domain and all A1 modules.
- Approximate size: 5 lessons, with Lesson 87 as the final A1 checkpoint.

## 7. Checkpoint and review strategy

- Lessons 42, 47, 52, 57, 62, 67, 72, 77 and 82 are local integration or
  review points. They should introduce little or no new material.
- Each local checkpoint should combine at least two earlier functions and
  provide deterministic evidence through comprehension, builder or typed work.
- Spoken practice can accompany every module but cannot be required for lesson
  mastery or checkpoint success.
- Lesson 87 is the only final A1 checkpoint. It must contain no new language
  and should use existing lesson/session evidence and recovery behavior.
- A failed checkpoint must remain retryable. Recovery should point to authored
  source steps or ordinary review, not create a new assessment subsystem.
- Course completion and A1 competency outcome must remain distinct. “All
  authored lessons complete” is not equivalent to proficiency certification.

## 8. Modality progression

Reading progresses from 3–4 sentence profiles and practical messages to 5–6
sentence notes, schedules, menus, timetables and short narratives. Questions
target explicit facts, order, time, preference, reason or directly supported
inference.

True listening progresses from one familiar sentence to two- or four-sentence
messages, short announcements and 3–5-turn exchanges. The current audio model
already supports a single approved asset with a multi-sentence transcript; the
transcript remains hidden until success, replay is explicit and semantic
choices remain Ukrainian.

Typed work progresses from one exact sentence to bounded two-sentence messages.
Exact answers, accepted answers and existing `prefix_with_value` contracts are
appropriate when the target structure is known. Free semantic writing is not
objective evidence.

Guided dialogues progress from one-function A0 exchanges to 2–3 connected
functions and finally 3–5 learner turns. Every learner turn must have a
turn-specific cue when actions differ, and the visible timeline must remain
causal: `I1 → L1 → I2 → L2 ...`.

Spoken practice increases steadily through listen-repeat, delayed imitation,
spoken recall and guided rehearsal. It remains practice-only under the current
runtime contract.

## 9. A1 vocabulary and recycling policy

The active vocabulary target is approximately 1,000–1,500 words/chunks by the
end of A1, treated as a planning range rather than a quota. Each lesson should
introduce only the vocabulary needed for its scenario. Prefer known grammar
with a small new lexical set, or a new bounded construction with mostly known
vocabulary.

Every new module must deliberately recycle at least two A0 domains. Recycling
must add delayed retrieval, a new modality, a new time frame, a new social
function or a new transfer demand. Repeating an A0 lesson with different nouns
is not sufficient.

| Typed production | SUPPORTED NOW for bounded exact/accepted/prefix structures; use separate steps when several independent facts are required |
| Connected writing | SUPPORTED WITH CONTENT ONLY for fixed/bounded two-sentence answers; multiple semantically free accepted messages are not objectively scorable and must be constrained or practice-only |
| Guided dialogue | SUPPORTED NOW for 3–5 learner turns, turn cues, exact/prefix contracts and interlocutor audio; author with explicit causal timelines |
| Spoken practice | SUPPORTED NOW as practice-only; never include it in objective checkpoint criteria |
| Checkpoint/recovery | SUPPORTED NOW through existing lesson/session retry, remediation and review; Spanish A1 should reuse current competency architecture if/when a catalog entry is registered |
| Persistence/review | SUPPORTED NOW; verify each new activity mix with existing resume/review tests |

No runtime extension is required before authoring Lessons 38–42. A later small
extension may be justified if A1 needs multiple accepted connected messages in a
single objectively scored field; until then, split the structure into
deterministic steps or keep freer writing practice-only.

## 11. Course size decision

- Minimum viable complete A1: 45 lessons (Lessons 38–82), provided every module
  has its integration lesson and the past-event module is included.
- Recommended: 50 lessons (Lessons 38–87), including the final integrated
  checkpoint and one complete five-lesson batch per module.
- Upper useful bound: approximately 60 lessons. Beyond that, add lessons only
  for demonstrated reinforcement or a genuinely missing communicative behavior;
  do not extend the course by splitting grammar or vocabulary into artificial
  units.

The recommended 50-lesson size follows from ten distinct capability domains,
one local integration point per domain and enough delayed recycling to prevent
A0/A1 constructions from being learned once and abandoned.

## 12. First implementation batch: Lessons 38–42

This is the best first batch because it crosses the A0→A1 boundary cleanly:
the learner already knows identity, home, time-adjacent numbers, transport and
basic places, while routine/frequency is a genuinely new high-frequency
capability. It introduces no tense paradigm or large vocabulary dump, supports
reading/listening/typed/guided/spoken work, and supplies time/frequency frames
that every later module needs.

- Lesson 38 — `Мій звичайний день`: describe a normal day with a bounded routine
  frame. New: `¿Qué haces normalmente?`, routine chunks. Recycle identity,
  home, transport. This establishes the first A1 personal-description behavior.
- Lesson 39 — `Коли це відбувається?`: ask and understand time/frequency. New:
  `¿A qué hora?`, `todos los días`. Recycle numbers, places and routine. This
  makes routine information actionable.
- Lesson 40 — `Ранок, день і вечір`: extract sequence from a short routine text
